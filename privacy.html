Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

WebTrigger is designed with privacy in mind. Most functionality operates entirely on your local network without requiring cloud services or user accounts.

Information We Collect

Current Versions

At this time, WebTrigger does not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Usage analytics
  • Browsing history
  • Device location
  • Action configurations

No user data is transmitted to WebTrigger servers.

Local Storage

WebTrigger stores configuration data locally on your computer, including:

  • Actions
  • Local Data Sources (LDS)
  • Themes and WebShells
  • Application settings
  • Device pairing information

This information remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

Community Content

If you download themes, plugins, or action packs created by other users, those items may contain code, scripts, or resources supplied by third parties.

Users should review community content before importing it.

Future Services

Future versions of WebTrigger may offer optional services such as:

  • Theme repositories
  • Plugin repositories
  • Update checking
  • Community sharing features

If those services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what information is collected and how it is used.

Security

WebTrigger includes security features such as:

  • Device pairing
  • Authentication tokens
  • Session management

However, no software can guarantee complete security.

Users remain responsible for securing their own networks and devices.
