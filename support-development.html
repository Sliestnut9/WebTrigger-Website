Every bug report, feature request, WebShell, screenshot, and piece of feedback helps make WebTrigger better. If you're enjoying the project, you're already helping by being here.

Help Power The Future Of WebTrigger!

I started working on WebTrigger because no existing software worked exactly the way I wanted it to. What began as a personal project quickly became something I realized others could enjoy and benefit from just as much as I do.

WebTrigger has already come a long way, but its potential grows with every idea, feature request, bug report, and piece of community feedback. There are still plenty of ideas left on the whiteboard, and one thing's for sure:

Bolt and I are just getting started.

If you'd like to support development, every contribution helps power the next update and bring new ideas to life.

WebTrigger exists because software should serve you - your setup, your workflow, your UI. Not the other way around.

I made this with heart for all the tinkerers who see an old phone, a spare tablet, or a weird workflow and think, "I can make that useful."
